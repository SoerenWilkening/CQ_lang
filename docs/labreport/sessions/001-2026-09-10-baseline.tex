\subsection*{What CQ is}
CQ is plain C plus one header, \fpath{include/CQ.h}. A value becomes quantum by passing through
\texttt{cq\_theta} (an \(R_y\) rotation, the \emph{sole} taint source); \texttt{cq\_measure} is the
sole sink; \texttt{cq\_phi} is a \emph{register-free} branch phase, \texttt{void cq\_phi(double)},
since 2026-09-06. Stock \texttt{clang -O1} compiles the source; one out-of-tree LLVM~19 module pass,
\texttt{cq-lowering}, propagates taint forward through ordinary arithmetic, control flow and calls
and lowers every tainted opcode to a reversible runtime template. The runtime is trace-only: each
gate prints one line, and the golden trace is the contract (Figure~\ref{fig:001:pipeline}). There is
no LLVM fork, no MLIR dialect, no parser, no \texttt{quantum} keyword, no simulator and no backend
yet: correctness is established by golden end-to-end diffs plus independent adversarial review
with analytic reasoning (\fpath{CLAUDE.md} Rules~2 and~13).

\subsection*{What a user can write today}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Straight-line quantum arithmetic.} Any stock integer/fp opcode, compare or cast on
    a tainted value, mixed with classical operands, at every C width the ABI names; the whole DAG of
    dead intermediates is uncomputed LIFO and freed only where provably \(|0\rangle\).
  \item \textbf{Tainted \texttt{if}.} A \emph{phase arm} (\texttt{if (q==5) cq\_phi(}\(\pi\)\texttt{)})
    lowers by \emph{kickback}: flag mint, one uncontrolled \texttt{cqrt\_rz\_i1} on the flag, strip,
    free. A \emph{value arm} (\texttt{if (c) q = q*3+1}) lowers by the \emph{controlled-copy merge}:
    the arm's pure chain is hoisted and run unconditionally, then merged into a fresh rail by two
    X-bracketed \texttt{cqrt\_copy\_<W>\_controlled}. A controlled \(R_y\) arm on a disjoint register,
    a controlled \emph{state prep} with a constant seed, the C \texttt{if/else} with one constant-angle
    rotation per arm (angle-select by difference), compound \texttt{\&\&}/\texttt{||}/\texttt{\^{}}
    conditions, the depth-2 homogeneous phase nest, sequential regions, a pure classical call under
    control, tainted \texttt{select} (integer and scalar fp data) and tainted \texttt{switch} in four
    shapes all lower. No arm class routes through \texttt{cqrt\_cswap} any more; its single minter is
    the 7.2 abort park (measured: one call site of \texttt{emitRoute}, one golden containing a cswap).
  \item \textbf{Loops.} Classically bounded loops lower \emph{rolled} (Case A in-place rail collapse,
    Case B per-iteration uncompute), including a non-exiting tainted phase region inside the body
    (Grover as a \texttt{for} loop) and an in-loop controlled \(R_y\) arm; a value-arm \texttt{break}
    lowers by capture and whole-loop reverse-play; the shift-count idiom is recognised as
    \texttt{llvm.ctlz}. A quantum-dependent trip count declines (Rule 18, narrowed to a derivation
    on 2026-08-21, unbuilt).
  \item \textbf{Functions and memory.} Plain C functions lift by clone-on-taint; const-global QROM
    reads and \texttt{alloca} Shadow store/load lower; controlled \texttt{return}/\texttt{abort} and
    the output tape exist. \texttt{math.h} headline coverage is 159/183 cells (87\%), composed of
    101/120 injection + 6/6 CQ-provided + 42/42 recognition keep-set + 10/12 macros + 0/3 by-design
    \texttt{nan} --- \emph{as published 2026-08-23 by the generator; not re-measured this session.}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{How correctness is established}
The defining hazard is the \emph{verify-clean silent miscompile}: IR that passes \texttt{opt -verify}
and prints a plausible trace but computes the wrong unitary. Four layers stand against it, and the
project's history says the fourth is the one that finds things:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{lit fixtures} (\fpath{ir-pass/test}), red-green with a decline fixture mirroring
    every diagnostic string character-for-character;
  \item \textbf{e2e golden traces} (\fpath{tests/e2e}), the only layer that links, plus stage 2b's
    rail-pairing check;
  \item \textbf{twin matrices} asserting byte-identity between two spellings of one program
    (rolled vs hand-unrolled, diamond vs pre-hoisted \texttt{select}, angle select vs by-difference);
  \item \textbf{independent adversarial Rule-13 review} with analytic reasoning. Every confirmed
    silent miscompile in the record was found here or by an e2e diff, never by the verifier.
\end{enumerate}
The rate is the fact to carry: the loop work hid over-accepts four increments in a row; the
register-free \texttt{cq\_phi} increment took eight findings over six adversarial rounds on one
function, two of them introduced by a fix; and the strongest single finding of the year is that
\emph{every shipped Grover golden before 2026-09-05 was the identity on \(q\)}, because the
per-qubit tensored \(R_z\) that controlled phase arms used is diagonal and fixes \(|0\ldots0\rangle\)
(\bd{3qlq}, PRD \S1). Nothing in layers 1--3 could see it; the traces were self-consistent records
of the identity.

\subsection*{Standing lessons the record keeps repeating}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Over-declining is safe; over-accepting is a silent miscompile.} A loud decline is a
    feature. Rule 17 adds the development posture: a decline is not the escape hatch when the task is
    to build the shape.
  \item \textbf{A stage that runs once has a round-2-false premise.} \texttt{repropagateControlMerges}
    is a second full round of control detection; two stages standing where a one-round pipeline put
    them were found wrong by review and by zero fixtures.
  \item \textbf{When you widen a set, ask what other set was computed over the same closure.} The
    rail-record fence widened its gates and not its records; the omission was found one round later.
  \item \textbf{A syntactic enumeration loses to InstCombine.} The state-prep seed fence F2 lost three
    rounds as a set of spellings and held once it became a semantic question (LVI over every live
    value, then forward abstract evaluation).
  \item \textbf{A number can measure the wrong program.} The retired 165/174 libm claim counted
    recognition as injection; the census later scored bodies openlibm does not build. Totals are now
    generated, printed with their composition, never hand-copied.
\end{itemize}
