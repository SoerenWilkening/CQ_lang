\subsection*{What the shape is, and what \texttt{-O1} does to the rest of the family}
\texttt{int a = 5; bool b0 = cq\_theta(b0, 0.5); if (b0 == 1) a -= 3;} is one \texttt{select} with
the register as its condition; so are \texttt{b ? q : 9}, \texttt{b ? q+1 : q}, \texttt{b ? 1.5 : 2.5},
\texttt{(b != c) ? 2 : 5}, and --- measured, not assumed --- \texttt{if (b) a = a + 3} on a quantum
\texttt{a}, which the previous session's probe family had filed as a \emph{value arm}: \texttt{-O1}
hoists the add and folds the diamond. A value arm survives as a diamond only from a five-op-or-longer
chain (\fpath{inc1-records/session-r9e5/SCOPE_B2.md} \S0.1). The 6.5 classical-call arm on a
\texttt{bool} \emph{is} plain-C reachable (\texttt{noinline}, pure callee, run-time argument) and its
\texttt{int} twin already compiles.

\subsection*{The design decision worth reading}
Step 4 of the copy merge fires the second copy on \emph{not}-flag, and the project's only spelling
of that is a self-inverse \texttt{cqrt\_x} bracket on the flag. Entry 002 established that a user's
register may never carry that bracket (three lowered-stream predicates depend on it), and escaped
with the diagonal identity \(X P(\theta) X = e^{i\theta} P(-\theta)\) --- which exists only for a
diagonal gate. A controlled basis \emph{copy} has no such form for its not-half. So the flag is
copied out of the register: \texttt{bf = cqrt\_alloc\_i1(0); cqrt\_cnot(h\_b, bf)}, the cleared
stream on \texttt{bf}, then \texttt{cqrt\_cnot(h\_b, bf); cqrt\_free(bf)}. After the copy the pair is
\(\sum_x \alpha_x |x\rangle_b |x\rangle_{bf}\); one copy fires per basis branch; \texttt{b} is untouched
(the copies write \texttt{out}, the brackets act on \texttt{bf}); the second CNOT returns \texttt{bf}
to \(x \oplus x = 0\) on every branch, so the free is a proven-\(|0\rangle\) free. The only gates
touching the register are read-only controls --- the gate class the entry-002 nest already emits.
Two alternatives were weighed and recorded (a bracket on the register; the identity
\(\neg b\cdot F = F \oplus b\cdot F\) with an uncontrolled copy, fewer gates but a disposition change
in two predicates written for the method-B copy-out) in \fpath{BRIEF_B1.md} \S1.
