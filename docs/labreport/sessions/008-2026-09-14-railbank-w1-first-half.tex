\subsection*{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \fconfirmed\ Three \emph{shipped} fixtures were pinning a wrong circuit.
        \texttt{control\_phase\_kickback\_mixed\_\{hoisted\_template,preheader\_volatile\_imm,short\_sext\}\_leak\_control.ll}
        each record \texttt{3/0 rolled and 3/3 unrolled} and each end
        \texttt{q = cq\_theta(q,0.9); cq\_measure(q)} --- a non-diagonal rotation on the very
        register the leaked rails copy --- then call that leak ``the PRIME-DIRECTIVE
        disposition \ldots\ the safe direction (Rule 6)''. Rule 6 governs whether a dirty rail
        may be \emph{freed}; Rule 8 governs this. Converted; \texttt{CLAUDE.md} \S5 corrected.
  \item \fconfirmed\ Their supporting evidence was a category error worth more than the bug:
        ``byte-identical rolled traces, \texttt{diff} empty --- that equality IS the assertion''
        compares the rolled form \emph{across two binaries}, while the \texttt{3/0} vs
        \texttt{3/3} on the same line is the rolled-vs-unrolled divergence. A stability
        measurement read as a correctness one.
  \item \fconfirmed\ Owner ruling D2 was re-ruled mid-session (D4) because Track S tested its
        premise before implementing and measured it false: \bd{s82e} is two classes, and the
        divergent half is a wrong unitary, not an accepted leak.
  \item \fconfirmed\ Two P1 over-accepts \emph{survive} the \bd{s82e} fence (\bd{c767},
        \bd{7845}); its benign/divergent separation holds by accident on the three programs it
        was built from. Coverage claims corrected in the \emph{source} at nine sites.
  \item \fconfirmed\ Blocking for the deferred half: \texttt{Bank} does not imply
        \emph{promoted} (\bd{8a3p}), and carve C6's own argument text is true of the promoted
        shape and false of the raw one --- the owner's own program. The store-move clause
        tests the GEP's block, not the load's (\bd{n74d}).
  \item \foverdecline\ \bd{2g52} --- a constant-trip loop with a volatile in-place op declines,
        though its rolled trace is byte-identical to the hand-unrolled twin's.
  \item \fconfirmed\ \texttt{free\_pairing\_check}'s census \emph{over}-counts as well as
        under-counts (\bd{rixk}): eleven lines of C, loop-free, report \texttt{6 rail mint(s)}
        for a trace that allocates 3. The figures \texttt{CLAUDE.md} \S4c cites are unaffected and are
        the regression test.
  \item \fconfirmed\ The committed \emph{value} witness for the F\,$\times$\,P composition is
        vacuous on its own reachable support (\bd{gzgs}): its fourth region multiplexes a flag

\end{itemize}
