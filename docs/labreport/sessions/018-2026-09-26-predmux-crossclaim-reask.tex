\subsection*{Goal}
Entry~017 landed the M4 placement conjunct at \texttt{PredMuxFlatten}'s E6 gate and closed a
verify-clean silent wrong unitary, but its own Rule-13 round~3 filed two P2s against the ground
that fix stands on. The first, \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-woa0}, is that the conjunct is a
\emph{point-in-time} property: it promises ``a \(\varphi\) \emph{this} flatten will not
convert'', which is true of the region being claimed and false of the pipeline, because one
region is claimed per call inside a fixpoint and nothing re-asks E6 about a region already
emitted. This session was to apply \texttt{CLAUDE.md}'s standing L1 cure to it.

\subsection*{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \fconfirmed\ The composition reproduces on the shipping binary exactly as round~3
        recorded it, and it lands on a loud decline only because \texttt{collectCondTree}
        refuses the resulting \texttt{xor}-of-\texttt{select} --- round~2's ``a downstream
        accident, not a fence'', verbatim. The re-ask replaces the accident with a named owner.
        \textbf{Not one verdict moved} across seven probes; five decline \emph{strings} moved.
  \item The fence \textbf{observes, it does not predict}. It fires on the select-chain
        conversion of a recorded \(\varphi\), not on a re-derivation of where that \(\varphi\)
        lives, so it can neither over-decline a \(\varphi\) that survives nor miss one that does
        not --- and it covers \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-0fly}'s region-merge cell without asking a
        placement question at all. \textbf{Covered is not closed}: 0fly asks for the unwritten
        dominance argument to be written down \emph{at the conjunct}, and the conjunct is
        unchanged.
  \item \textbf{The mutation matrix found a missing fixture, which is what mutation is for.}
        Two of three mutants pinned what was expected (drop the record, or drop the commit, and
        the decline fixture goes red in both prefixes --- verified by \emph{running} FileCheck,
        not by re-reading prefixes). The third reported that the \texttt{!BoolAlg} conjunct was
        \textbf{unpinned}: removing it moves \textbf{0 of 2156} fixtures. Chasing that produced
        a shipped accept fixture for a cell no sweep could have shown --- a recorded \(\varphi\)
        converted by the boolean-algebra path, which must \emph{not} be a violation.
  \item \fconfirmed\ \textbf{A probe that contains half its shape.} The first construction of
        that witness recorded the \(\varphi\) and then never converted it: the outer region left
        at \texttt{gate=W-witness}, because one merge \(\varphi\) with two incomings satisfies no
        claim witness. Caught by reading the debug trace rather than the verdict
        (\bd{test\_C\_libtooling-46oo}'s class); a second merge \(\varphi\) is what closes it,
        and the fixture header says so.
  \item \textbf{A control that reports a pass without reporting its denominator is not a
        control.} The mutant null control first printed \texttt{divergent=0 of 1}: under
        \texttt{zsh} an unquoted \texttt{\$FILES} is one word, so the loop ran once and ``0
        divergent'' was true and worthless. Only the printed denominator caught it --- the same
        class as round~3's \texttt{export -f} bashism, one shell feature over.
  \item \textbf{\texttt{CLAUDE.md} \S5 was stale by three landings and is corrected by
        measurement.} It still said the owner's \texttt{grover\_oracle\_uncompute.c} does not
        compile and that increments 3, 4 and 5 remain. Measured on both binaries this session:
        \texttt{opt} exit 0, \textbf{0 bytes of stderr}, 124 lowered lines, stage~2b \texttt{3
        verified, 0 violation(s), 0 unproven}. The 2026-09-24 and 2026-09-25 landings have no
        \S4 section of their own; their record of record is named in \S5 instead.
\end{itemize}
