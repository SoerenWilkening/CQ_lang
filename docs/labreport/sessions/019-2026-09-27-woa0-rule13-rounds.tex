\subsection*{Method, and the one deviation}
Two agents, neither of which wrote \commit{357f2528}, neither of which read the other's report,
on disjoint primary surfaces: round~A on the completeness of the \textsc{record} and the
\textsc{observation}, round~B on the \textsc{re-ask}'s lifetime, slot and false-positive
direction. Both returned \texttt{CLEAR-WITH-ITEMS} with \textbf{0 P1}.

The rounds ran \emph{concurrently on a frozen subject}, not sequentially. The precedent behind
``budget two'' is entry~017's round~2 finding a P1 inside round~1's \emph{fix}; that value
exists only when a fix lands between the rounds, and none did. Concurrency bought
disjoint-surface coverage and gave up nothing that was in play. Any fix for the findings below
owes a further round, so the property is preserved where it bites.

\subsection*{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \foverdecline\ \textbf{Both rounds converged, independently, on the same defect} ---
        \texttt{F-RA-1} and \texttt{F-RB-1}, four different exhibits, from opposite
        assignments. \texttt{Leaves} is committed at the E6 \emph{admission} while
        \texttt{run()} mints edge predicates \emph{lazily}, so a rail \(\varphi\) can be
        recorded and never read; a later claim's vacuous \textsc{rauw} then declines a module
        the oracle compiles correctly. Both rounds built the one-line \texttt{!P->use\_empty()}
        gate and \textbf{both measured it insufficient} --- it recovers the never-minted
        sub-class and not the minted-then-dead one. \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-707a}; not fixed
        here, because narrowing a decline is an accept-direction change.
  \item \fdoc\ \textbf{The reason it was invisible is worth more than the defect.} Of the
        change's eight probes, \emph{both accepting rows commit nothing}, so its probe matrix
        has \textbf{zero cells} in the quadrant ``record populated \(\wedge\) module accepts'',
        and the corpus cannot supply one (5 commit modules in 2157, none converts). A clean
        A\(\rightarrow\)D\,=\,0 measured over a population with no members of the
        class is not evidence about the class --- this project's own standing caveat, not
        applied to the row that needed it. Round~A found it by running the implementer's
        instrument over the implementer's probe set.
  \item \fdoc\ The central safety sentence, \emph{``it cannot be wrong in either direction''},
        is false; it is one-sided. Its two sibling sentences are \textbf{literally true and
        answer the wrong question} --- in every exhibit the \(\varphi\) \emph{was} converted
        and did \emph{not} survive --- which is how the gap survived three careful re-readings.
  \item \fdoc\ \fpath{ir-pass/src/PredMuxFlatten.cpp}:11's \emph{``PredMux can only widen the
        accept surface''} is now measurably false. It is \textbf{the other header in the file
        that \commit{357f2528} half-fixed}, while citing ``a correction not applied to the
        source is not a correction'' as its reason for fixing the first.
  \item \fdoc\ The stated pre-fence mechanism --- \emph{``\texttt{collectCondTree} refuses the
        resulting \texttt{xor}-of-\texttt{select}''}, at six sites including entry~018's own
        text --- is measurably false. There is no \texttt{xor} in the post-flatten module on
        either binary; the owner is \texttt{SelectClassify}'s data-select grammar.
  \item \fpreexisting\ The pre-emptive slot \textbf{masks a genuine \texttt{cq taint escape}},
        constructed. Entry~018's ``0 such cells measured; not proved absent'' now has its
        counterexample. \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-nkgt}; the masking half is an owner question.
  \item \fpreexisting\ The record is keyed on SSA identity and spans two \emph{cloning}
        pre-passes; identity stability across them is argued nowhere. Argued, not constructed.
        \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-tzw2}.
  \item \status{Retired claim:} \textbf{a \texttt{+Asserts} build is a four-fixture bill, not a
        structural impossibility}. Round~B built one and swept 2157 fixtures: 4 aborts, exactly
        round~3's four, and woa0 adds none. \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-r4f6}. That is the gate the
        unchecked-\texttt{cast<>} family --- the family entry~017's P1 came from --- has never
        had.
\end{itemize}
