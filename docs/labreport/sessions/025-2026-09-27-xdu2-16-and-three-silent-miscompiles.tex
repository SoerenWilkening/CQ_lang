\subsection*{Goal}
Implement the two follow-ups entry 024 left: \bd{xdu2.16}, the loop-layer wall
in front of \bd{xdu2.8}'s program (shared matrix row 13), and \bd{9bhg}, the
over-decline the \bd{3jru} fence introduced. The two were independent and ran
in parallel. \bd{9bhg}'s review rounds then kept exposing older silent
miscompiles that its wider acceptance would have reached, so on the owner's
instruction those were fixed on \texttt{main} first.

\subsection*{What landed}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \commit{def1b088} --- \bd{xdu2.16} --- a compound condition tree feeding a
    4.4 select in a rolled loop compiles: when the select emitter erases a
    round-1 invariant root that \texttt{-O1} hoisted, the loop layer re-derives
    the grant at final taint instead of refusing. Row 13 compiles; \bd{xdu2.8}
    closed. Two review rounds, no P1.
  \item \commit{ba80bda1} --- \bd{d88e} --- the one-register merge-phi lift is
    recorded and asked again after \texttt{specialize()}; a base the specializer
    proved classical now declines with the hand-inlined twin's verdict. Two
    review rounds, no P1.
  \item \commit{aee8f4f8} --- \bd{8e5r}, \bd{um24}, \bd{j8sk} --- every
    pointer-keyed table of the taint set cleans itself when LLVM deletes a key
    (\texttt{ScrubOnDelete.h}); a test-only allocator driver
    (\texttt{cq-opt-debugalloc}) makes the aliasing reproducible; a
    compiler-AST guard refuses new raw-pointer members. Four review rounds.
  \item \commit{c1136be2} --- \bd{vsfp} and 16 beads found while fixing it ---
    one shared liveness predicate decides whether a call argument is read and
    whether a value is dead. It drives a dead-argument drop before
    specialization, dead-cone deletion (including after volatile-store
    erasure), the M-C reuse check, and a post-specialize neutraliser with a
    backstop. Six fix rounds, ten reviews; the last cleared with no P1.
  \item \commit{cf1f84b2} --- \bd{9bhg} closed as fixed by \bd{vsfp}: the
    original program is pinned as an accept fixture; the 9bhg branch was not
    merged. Residual: \bd{ev3m}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \fconfirmed\ \fpreexisting\ \bd{d88e}: the lift rewrote a register phi to
    a call result the specializer later proved classical, deleting the user's
    \texttt{cq\_measure} (or, in one variant, measuring the wrong rail). Found
    by a review of \bd{9bhg}, reproduced on the frozen \texttt{main} binary.
  \item \fconfirmed\ \fpreexisting\ \bd{8e5r}: stages erased tainted instructions
    without removing them from the taint set; a specialized clone's classical
    instruction allocated at a freed address was lowered as quantum, with a
    constant used as a register handle. The frozen \texttt{main} binary was
    wrong on the reviewer's \texttt{k65} program in 7 of 10 runs.
  \item \fconfirmed\ \fpreexisting\ \bd{vsfp}: a specialized clone that ignores
    an argument still received the caller's derived which-path rail, which was
    never uncomputed. For \texttt{b10f}, rotations of 0.5 then 0.7 give
    $P(b{=}1)=\cos^2 0.6 \approx 0.681$; the compiled circuit gave 0.836,
    because the leftover copy dephases the register. Exit 0, no warning.
  \item \fconfirmed\ Fix rounds introduced defects that later review rounds
    caught before landing: two compiler crashes (\bd{1xp9}, \bd{f061}), a
    segfaulting executable from a nulled \texttt{byval} argument (\bd{91k9}),
    stale taint after a fold (\bd{otam}), an unsound exemption (\bd{tzfa}), a
    missed \texttt{\#pragma clang section} form (\bd{qce6}) and a regression
    through a \texttt{musttail} forward (\bd{w7nm}). None reached \texttt{main}.
  \item \foverdecline\ After \bd{vsfp}, \bd{9bhg}'s path is reachable from C
    only through \texttt{musttail} forwarders, measured by its implementer;
    the owner chose to close it rather than land the machinery (\bd{ev3m}).
\end{itemize}
