\subsection*{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \fconfirmed\ \fpreexisting\ \bd{fmt3}: \texttt{clang -O1} pinned a register
    to a literal on an inner branch, merged the two now-identical
    \texttt{cq\_theta} calls and deleted the branch. The pass then saw IR
    byte-identical to a legitimate literal prep (probes \texttt{n13} and
    \texttt{n12} lower to identical modules), so it compiled a prep where the
    source rotated a live register. No pass-side fence can separate the two;
    the owner chose the frontend fix.
  \item \fconfirmed\ \fpreexisting\ \bd{4e4p}, \bd{uw5u}: the fix above does not
    reach a call through a function pointer (clang puts \texttt{nomerge} on call
    sites only) or the fold of \texttt{cq\_theta((t==s)?t:s, $\theta$)} to
    \texttt{cq\_theta(s, $\theta$)}. Both open.
  \item \fconfirmed\ \fpreexisting\ \bd{9oob}: a library that defines its own
    \texttt{\_\_cq\_q\_*\_unc} twin with a wrong body is trusted as the adjoint;
    stage 2b reports it clean. Open.
  \item \fconfirmed\ Review rounds caught two regressions the fixes introduced
    before they landed: \bd{kyy1} (rhk5 round 1 lost a discharge the oracle made)
    and \bd{fb5y} (a module-defined \texttt{cq\_template\_*} was trusted by name
    and dirty rails were freed with no warning).
  \item \foverdecline\ Introduced by \bd{fmt3}'s option B and accepted by the
    owner: \bd{4l5q}, \bd{cx6j}. Filed: \bd{ur72}, \bd{w109}, \bd{nivh},
    \bd{y2b3}.
  \item \fdoc\ An owner ruling (the ``$\alpha$ extension'' for erased inner
    branches) was granted on a premise that a probe falsified within the hour,
    and was withdrawn; the cost of \texttt{nomerge} was first stated too high
    (the ternary spelling still reaches the angle select). Both corrected in the
    bead notes and \texttt{CLAUDE.md} \S4l.
  \item \fdoc\ \bd{2dfk} found that \texttt{e2e-spec-scrub-dangling-key} had run
    without its \texttt{CQ\_OPT\_DEBUG\_ALLOC=lifo} setting since
    \commit{818f8afa}; fixed.
\end{itemize}
