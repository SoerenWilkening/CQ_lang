\subsection*{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \fconfirmed\ (pre-existing, fixed) Reverse-play returns a rail to its
  \emph{birth} state. A \texttt{cqrt\_alloc\_<W>(seed)} root is born
  $|\mathit{seed}\rangle$, so \texttt{alloc(m); ry; ry\textsuperscript{-1}; free}
  reset $|m\rangle$. Clause (a) of the 261j invariant (``R starts at
  $|0\rangle$'') was never asked of roots. About twenty fixtures and three
  slice headers licensed the free as ``disentangled and in a known classical
  state'', which is measurement immunity and not the $|0\rangle$ proof
  \texttt{cqrt\_free} requires.
  \item \fconfirmed\ (instrument gap, filed) stage 2b's (A1)/(A2) certify an
  alloc rail returned to any classical value; it verified the dirty free.
  \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-fvba}.
  \item \fconfirmed\ (L2, filed) widening \texttt{FrozenRails} masks 28
  fixtures whose ``root never freed'' pins were written for another freeze on
  a runtime-seeded root. \bd{test\_C\_libtooling-bhi3}.
  \item Review: three rounds. R1 and R2 found no soundness hole (seven probes
  in all); R1--R3 found fixture windows narrower than their prose and stale
  documentation, all fixed before the gate.
\end{itemize}
