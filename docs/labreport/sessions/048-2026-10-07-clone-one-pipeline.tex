\subsection*{Goal}
Finish \bd{3c6m.73} and \bd{p3kd.7.8}: admit FMA with a classical product and
quantum addend, then lower lifted classical callees with main's stages. The clone
pipeline had omitted loop and control mechanisms that main already ran. It also
retyped FP parameters before those stages could inspect their well-typed source
operations. This continuation reviewed the prepared implementation, merged it,
and measured acceptance through the user-facing driver.

\subsection*{Findings and correctness argument}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \fconfirmed\ \bd{p3kd.7.10}, pre-existing: early FP-to-i32 retyping
  made the old clone select emitter choose integer copies for FP inputs. Copies
  at i32 truncated FP64/FP80 data while LLVM verification passed. The coshl golden
  had pinned this error. Deferred retyping and shared SelectMergeEmit repair it;
  the P0 issue is closed after merge and the saved-IR audit below.
  \item Strip elision depends on basis permutations and emitter ownership.
  After a scratch rail's last read, an omitted strip writes only that rail; the
  result copy is unchanged. Retaining scratch lets the exact mirror undo the
  entire forward sequence. Owners must forbid a later read after the scratch
  free. Borrowed parameters never enter the scratch-free list. Nonzero classical
  materializations remain allocated under the existing policy.
  \item Four independent review rounds accepted, using 6, 6, 6 and 2 fresh
  lowering executions. They attacked theta-root assumptions H1--H7, aliased
  parameters, FP signature replacement and attributes, clone flags, late reads
  of conditions, nested mirrors, and round-2 clones. The last round witnessed
  both a newly discovered candidate and mask growth from 10 to 11 with QQ XOR.
  One round-2 probe was malformed and one QROM probe had weakened integer-root
  coverage; neither earns the intended coverage. Later rounds filled the FP80
  and round-2 reachability gaps. Inputs, outputs and reports are committed under
  \fpath{docs/design/clone-one-pipeline-review/}.
  \item \fdoc\ Static unrolling can mutate the reachable original-callee cone
  under its U1--U8 checks; prediction metadata cleanup is module-wide. The
  StageList scope comment now names these exceptions. An OpcodeLowering comment
  also incorrectly called clone loop marks inert; the shared stages make its
  existing unconditional recheck necessary. These were comment corrections;
  no behavioral change followed green.
\end{itemize}
