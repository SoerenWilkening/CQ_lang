\subsection*{Findings}
Source inspection used Bennett.jl at
\texttt{824458d10e879c3fef673c231a02e5461dac667f} and BennettVM.jl at
\texttt{23d28e6471f9cf5d8a22177d08260ca86adfe841}. These are source evidence
pins; BennettVM's documentary upstream validation pin is \texttt{f904d0d}.
The two newly inspected heads were not built together.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \fdoc\ BennettVM ADR 0022 and its multi-use PHI regression require
  non-destructive transfers: preserve an incoming with later uses, and capture
  all parallel inputs before assigning destinations. Its generic edge/PHI
  ingest and genuine nested-loop fixture provide concrete lowering references.
  \item \fdoc\ Current VM source supports field-wise inner aggregate returns,
  heterogeneous insert-bits chains and consumed-sret reconciliation. Its
  historical coverage matrix still describes some of these paths as deferred.
  Capability claims must follow the pinned source and relevant tests.
  \item \fdoc\ The literal-certification regression records wrong forward
  answers that reversed cleanly. A classical round-trip alone cannot establish
  the forward semantics or CQ's unitary; the existing R2 evidence remains
  necessary. The VM's Float32 rejection, bit-model freeze treatment and
  checkpoint-replay SoftCall do not settle CQ's poison or f32/f80 circuit gaps.
\end{itemize}
