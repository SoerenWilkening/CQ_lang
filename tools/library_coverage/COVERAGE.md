Each `B(...)`/`St(...)` also carries a **`backend=`** tier (🟩 `standard` · 🟨 `synthesizable` · 🟥 `open` · `n/a`) — a static per-fn judgment of the emitted templates' reversible-backend availability, orthogonal to whether the fn lowers.
